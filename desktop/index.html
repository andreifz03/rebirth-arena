REBIRTH ARENA // COMMUNITY CHALLENGE
A NEW WAY TO PLAY YOUR MATCHES.
Rebirth Arena is an optional community challenge layered on top of completed VALORANT matches. It does not change matchmaking, skill rating, rewards or gameplay. It simply turns specific milestone eliminations into a long-term collection and competition system.
Omen
1
Play normally

Your own selected agent does not receive Rebirth points. You play VALORANT exactly as you normally would.

10
Milestone elimination

Rebirth Arena checks your exact 10th, 20th, 30th elimination and every later multiple of ten.

+1
The enemy gets the point

If your 10th elimination is against Reyna, Reyna receives one Rebirth point in your profile.

5
Rank up that agent

Collect five milestone points against the same enemy agent to advance that agent to the next Rebirth tier.

Example
The rule is based on the enemy you eliminate, not the character you are playing.
You are playing Phoenix→Your #10 kill defeatsREYNA→REYNA +1
There is no cash prize, in-game reward, ranked advantage or gameplay benefit for earning Rebirth points. The goal is simply to create a fun, long-term challenge and a social leaderboard for players who choose to participate.
Community competition
Once Riot account sync is enabled, opted-in players can compare their Rebirth journey on global leaderboards.
